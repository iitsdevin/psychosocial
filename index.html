Workplace Psychosocial Risk Assessment

A comprehensive tool to evaluate psychosocial hazards in your workplace

About This Assessment

This assessment helps identify and evaluate psychosocial risks in your workplace. Psychosocial hazards are aspects of work design, organization, and management that can cause psychological or physical harm.

What We Assess:

  • Job Demands: Workload, time pressure, and emotional demands
  • Control & Autonomy: Decision-making authority and task flexibility
  • Manager Support: Guidance, feedback, and assistance from supervisors
  • Peer Support: Collaboration and support from colleagues
  • Relationships: Workplace culture, respect, and conflict management
  • Role Clarity: Understanding of responsibilities and expectations
  • Change Management: How organizational changes are communicated and managed

Instructions:

Rate each statement based on your experience over the past 3 months. Be honest - your responses will help identify areas for improvement.
